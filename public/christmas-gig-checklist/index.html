Your between-shifts checklist

Tap each item as you set it up. Four small stacks, all free to start, all doable from the driver's seat or the couch between gigs.

  • 1 — Surveys in the gaps. Join a paid survey panel and answer on your phone while you wait for the next ping, order, or pickup. Start with our base guide, Surveys That Pay.
  • 2 — Points on planned shopping. Route the gift shopping you were already doing through a rewards site, so the spend earns points back instead of nothing.
  • 3 — Receipts from every run. After grocery and supply runs, scan the receipts for cash back — a rebate layer on trips you already took.
  • 4 — A bridge for late payouts. When a platform payout lands after the gift deadline, a cash-advance app can bridge the gap between gig payouts — read its terms first and borrow only what the next payout covers.

Keep the checklist honest

  • Write the gift list first. Decide who you are buying for and the most you will spend per gift — before the season starts.
  • The fund is the ceiling. When the stacked cash runs out, shopping is done. That rule is the whole strategy.
  • Skip anything that charges you to join. Legitimate panels are free; they are paid by brands, not by you.
  • A bridge is not income. An advance moves your own payout earlier — treat it as timing help, never as extra money, and read the terms before you tap.
