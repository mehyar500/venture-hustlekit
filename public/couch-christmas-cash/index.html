Keep it honest, keep it gentle

  • Write the gift list first. Decide who you are buying for and the most you will spend per gift — before shopping starts.
  • Cash is the ceiling. When the fund runs out, shopping is done. That rule is the whole strategy.
  • Skip anything that charges you to join. Legitimate panels are free; they are paid by brands, not by you.
  • Ignore full-time-income promises. The honest ceiling here is pocket money — stacked through December, that is exactly a gift fund.
