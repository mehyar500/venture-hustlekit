Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

When you buy HustleKit we collect your email address and the answers you give in the intake form (track, skills, hours, income goal, experience level, niche interest). Payment details are handled by Stripe — we never see or store your card number.

How we use it

Your intake answers are sent to our AI provider solely to generate your personalized playbook, then stored with your order so you can re-download it. Your email is used to deliver the playbook and purchase-related messages. We don't sell your data, and we don't use your answers for marketing without permission.

AI processing

Playbook generation runs on Cloudflare Workers AI. Your intake text is processed to produce your deliverable and is not used to train models.

Data retention & deletion

We keep order records as long as needed to serve your download link and for tax records. To request deletion of your data, email info@mehyar.us — there are no accounts on this product, so email is the deletion path.

Cookies

This site doesn't use tracking cookies. Cloudflare may set security cookies as part of serving the site.
